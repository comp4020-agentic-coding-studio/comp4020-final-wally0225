# Crit 8 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough was asking what my app does better than the group chat, instead of what my old pitch could grow into. Vybe started as an course group pitch full of profiles, AI matching and trust scores, all there to build a platform. Once I compared it to the hall or class group chat, the real problems were small and specific: asking is awkward, messages get buried, you can't see who's in, and people forget when and where. Those four became what the app does, and most of the pitch became what I chose not to build.

The second step came from thinking about how a student's day actually works. Invites first closed at their start time, but someone finishing a class 30 minutes late should still be able to come. Letting people join late, up to an hour before the end, came from that scenario, not from a feature list.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

In my earlier crits I learned to start from an MVP and to be the first user of what I build. This week added deciding what not to build. The agent could build any feature I asked for in minutes, so the hard part was no longer building; it was choosing. I want to be the developer who decides what good means for real people and can explain why, and who uses the agent to build and test those decisions rather than letting it fill the app with the median answer.
